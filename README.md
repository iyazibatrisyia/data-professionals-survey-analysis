# data-professionals-survey-analysis
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing career paths, compensation, job satisfaction, and entry barriers for 630+ data professionals worldwide.
## 💡 Overview & Executive Summary

Understanding the landscape of data careers—ranging from compensation expectations to entry friction—is crucial for aspiring professionals and industry leaders alike. This project transforms raw survey response data into key operational metrics and strategic visual insights using **Power BI**.

### 🗝️ Key Insights Identified
* **Demographics:** Analyzed global representation with major response clusters from the **United States** and **India**, with an overall average respondent age of **29.87 years**.
* **Compensation Trends:** Data Scientists and Data Engineers report the highest average salaries, while Data Analysts and Database Developers fall in the mid-range spectrum.
* **Tech Stack Preferences:** **Python** overwhelmingly dominates as the favorite programming language across roles, followed by **R** and **JavaScript**.
* **Job Satisfaction:** Overall satisfaction with Work/Life Balance rated at **5.74 / 10**, whereas Salary Satisfaction trailed slightly lower at **4.27 / 10**.
* **Entry Difficulty:** Over **67%** of respondents reported that breaking into the data industry was either **"Difficult"** or **"Very Difficult"**.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated

* **Business Intelligence Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation & ETL:** Power Query (Data cleaning, handling missing values, standardizing categories)
* **Data Modeling & DAX:** Custom measures for aggregations, weighted averages, and conditional dynamic counts
* **Visualization Techniques:**
  * **Treemap:** Geographic distribution of survey takers
  * **Clustered Bar Chart:** Average Salary by Job Title
  * **Gauge Visuals:** Work/Life Balance & Salary Satisfaction Indices
  * **Donut Chart:** Entry difficulty distribution Breakdown
  * **Stacked Bar Chart:** Programming language popularity by job title
